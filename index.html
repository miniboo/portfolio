Copilot Discover

A daily brief made just for you.

Copilot Discover is a consumer experience across the desktop app and One Copilot, designed as an agentic experience centered on human voices.

Its personalized daily brief draws on your chat history and memory, bringing together content from current publishers and AI-curated sources. Real voices and clear provenance stay at the heart of each story, so you know who’s behind it.

A generative UI model presents the content and adds augments to stories, with the aim of adapting the format, length, and knowledge level to what you need in the moment. The guiding intent is to earn your trust with every story.
